\section{Prompt Design}
\label{sec:prompts}

Each prompt comprises a system prompt that sets the analyst role and the strict-rule
context, and a user prompt that contains the formal bound and the explicit lists of
allowed, required, and forbidden claims. Three prompt variants are compared in a
20-sample pilot at the start of the evaluation block.

\section{Evaluation Protocol}
\label{sec:protocol}

The evaluation produces five hundred explanations per model and per event type, for a
target of 2\,500 explanation pairs across five models and five event types. Each
explanation is processed by \textsc{ReasonGuard} and labelled with its violation
codes and severity. Two hundred explanation pairs are manually annotated for ground
truth, and per-class precision, recall, and $F_{1}$ are reported. A subset of fifty
explanations is independently labelled by a VUT Brno domain expert at the M4
milestone, and inter-rater agreement is reported as Cohen's $\kappa$.
