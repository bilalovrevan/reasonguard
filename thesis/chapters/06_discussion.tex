\section{Threats to Validity}
\label{sec:discussion-threats}

\paragraph{Construct validity.}
\textsc{ReasonGuard} treats the formal verdict as a bound on what an explanation may
claim. If the formal verdict is itself incomplete or proxy in nature, the bound is
correspondingly weaker. Throughout the thesis the proxy fields are clearly labelled
and the analysis is conditional on the documented bound.

\paragraph{Internal validity.}
The V1--V5 detector is rule-based and therefore deterministic given a fixed bound and
a fixed explanation. The principal internal-validity concern is the manual annotation
of the 200-pair ground-truth set, which is subject to annotator bias. The
inter-rater agreement with the VUT Brno domain expert in Section~\ref{sec:results-kappa}
mitigates this concern.

\paragraph{External validity.}
The empirical evaluation is conducted on IEC-104 and Modbus traffic from VUT Brno
testbeds. Generalisation to other protocols and to non-ICS settings is not claimed,
although the framework is protocol-agnostic by construction.
